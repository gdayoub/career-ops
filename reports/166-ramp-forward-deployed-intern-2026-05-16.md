## B — Role Description

Ramp's Forward Deployed Engineering (FDE) team is a high-paced, customer-facing engineering team that works directly with Ramp's largest enterprise accounts to solve technical problems and deliver custom solutions.

**Responsibilities:**
- Design and build solutions to support Ramp's largest customers and scale its enterprise motion
- Interact directly with customers to understand pain points and shape product decisions
- Design and implement scalable systems from end to end
- Solve technical issues for Ramp's strategic accounts, configure integrations, ensure customer satisfaction
- Deliver solutions end to end: from understanding the problem through designing and shipping the system

**Required Qualifications:**
- Pursuing a BS or higher in Computer Science or related technical field
- Expected graduation between Dec 2026 and 2028
- Track record of shipping high-quality products/features OR portfolio of side projects
- Ability to turn business and product ideas into engineering solutions
- Desire to work in a fast-paced environment

**Preferred:**
- Experience with Python, TypeScript, APIs, integrations
- Customer-facing or consulting engineering experience
