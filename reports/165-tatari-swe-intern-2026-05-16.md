## B — Role Description

Tatari is a platform for TV advertising measurement and analytics, helping brands buy, measure, and optimize linear and streaming TV campaigns using ML-powered media intelligence.

**Responsibilities:**
- Join one of the backend engineering teams and contribute to development of the media buying and measurement platform
- Design, build, and improve product features with direct customer impact
- Work with backend and data teams to develop new features, analytics, and data systems
- Focus areas: systems tooling, data automation, data analysis and statistics, transforming real-world data into results
- The Media Intelligence team builds AI/ML systems for automated media planning, audience targeting, and prediction models

**Required Qualifications:**
- Pursuing BS or MS in Computer Science or related field
- Completed minimum two years of college/university
- Returning to school after the internship
- Python proficiency (or similar language)
- Basic understanding of relational databases and SQL (MySQL, Postgres)
- Commitment of minimum 10 weeks between May and August
