## A. Overview

Anthropic's STEM Fellows Program brings students and domain experts from STEM fields into Anthropic for ~4 months to work alongside research teams. Fellows pair their domain expertise with frontier models to evaluate, improve, and apply Claude's capabilities in their field.

**Program structure:**
- Duration: ~4 months (rolling cohorts: January, May, July, September 2027)
- Compensation: $3,850/week (~$96/hr) + $15,000/month in compute credits
- Location: Remote
- Tracks available: AI Safety, AI Security, ML Systems & Performance, Reinforcement Learning, The Anthropic Institute (Economics/Policy)

**Key requirements:**
- Strong Python programming ability
- STEM background with domain expertise
- No prior ML experience required — scientific judgment and ability to learn quickly matter more
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent (open to students)
- Rolling applications until spots fill
