## B — Role Description

The Strategy, Execution & Analytics team uses data to surface opportunities and ensure accurate interpretation across Coinbase's product and engineering orgs.

**Responsibilities:**
- Design and implement robust data pipelines ensuring data integrity and accessibility for analytics and ML initiatives
- Build scalable analytics models and systems alongside the analytics engineering team
- Develop and maintain scalable data infrastructure leveraging Python and cloud-based technologies
- Collaborate with business partners in product and engineering to identify key opportunities

**Required Qualifications:**
- Currently pursuing a degree or certificate in Computer Science, Data Engineering, or related discipline
- Experience or coursework in Python with strong understanding of data structures and algorithms
- Understanding of data analysis and database concepts with SQL experience
- Familiarity with or strong interest in AI, LLMs, and API integrations

**Nice to Have:**
- Exposure to cloud platforms (AWS, GCP, Azure)
- Understanding of software architecture principles (microservices, distributed systems, cloud-native design)
